About Secure Vault

Secure Vault is a web portal to store and access your personal files from different devices.

Why did we make it?

People often need important files when they are away from their own device. Using messaging apps to store files is inconvenient and logging in to them on another device can risk our privacy.

Secure Vault gives you one simple and safe place to keep your files.

What can you do?

Register and Login

Create your own account and login with your email and password.

Upload

Upload documents, notes, certificates, images and other files.

Manage

Search, view, download or delete your files from My Files page.

Who can use it?

  • Students
  • Teachers
  • Other working professionals
